Privacy Policy

Faith Words · Last updated 21 November 2026

Faith Words is a Christian crossword game. This page explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Playing as a guest

You can play without an account. In that case your progress is saved only on your own device, and we do not receive it.

If you create an account

To save your progress across devices, join leagues and play online, you can create an account. We then store:

  • Your email address and a password (stored securely by our sign-in provider, never in readable form), used only to sign you in.
  • Your display name and the optional title you choose to wear. These are visible to other players on leaderboards, in leagues, in groups and in online matches.
  • Your game data: level, points, coins, streaks, badges, verse album, settings, groups you create or join, weekly league standing, challenges and online match results.

We do not ask for your real name, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone, and we do not collect advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To run the game: saving progress, checking results fairly, showing leaderboards and matching you with opponents.
  • To send the daily verse reminder, only if you turn it on. Reminders are scheduled on your own device and use no personal data from our servers.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use it for tracking across other apps or websites.

Who handles your data

Account and game data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. The website is hosted on Vercel. The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts and a code library from jsDelivr, so those services can see your IP address when the page loads, as with any website. We do not share your data with anyone else except where the law requires it.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the game: open Me → Account → Delete my account and confirm. This permanently removes your account, progress, verse album, groups you own, league entries and challenges from our servers. Your display name is also removed from other players' past match records. You can also ask us to delete your data by contacting us below.

Children

Faith Words is a family-friendly game, but accounts are intended for people aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are never stored in readable form, and game scores and rewards are checked by our server so they cannot be altered from a player's device. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions or data requests: open an issue at github.com/PraiseKol/faithwords/issues. Please do not post private information in a public issue; ask for a private way to reach us and we will reply.
